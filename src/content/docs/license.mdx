Spool is free software. You can use, study, change and share it under the terms of the <a href={site.license.url}>{site.license.name}</a>, known as the AGPL. Module versions that were published under the Apache License 2.0 keep that license, and the `pom.xml` of each version on Maven Central states which license it has.

## Using Spool inside your organisation

Running Spool to move and process your own data is free, and the AGPL asks nothing of you in return. This is also true if you change the code of Spool, as long as the changed version is only used by people inside your organisation.

## When you have to share your code

The AGPL applies when Spool reaches people outside your organisation. If you distribute a product that includes Spool, or you let others use a service built on it over a network, you have to offer them the complete source code of that product or service under the AGPL. This covers your own code that runs together with Spool as one program, not only the changes you made to Spool.

## Commercial license

If you want to include Spool in a product or service without publishing your code, you can get a commercial license. Write to <a href={`mailto:${site.license.commercialContactEmail}`}>{site.license.commercialContactEmail}</a> and explain how you plan to use Spool.
